South Africa Travel Guide

Where to go

Bush, mountain, vineyard, coast.

A working South Africa itinerary balances three big ideas: a Big Five safari in the north, a few days in Cape Town with the mountain, and a slow drive east along the Garden Route. Johannesburg is a transit stop in and out; the Winelands are a long day trip from the city.

How to use this

It's a starting point.

Each region page has the things actually walked into — somewhere to stay, what to do, where to eat. The itinerary shows the exact day-by-day if it helps you pace.

The map plots everything in one place; the logistics page covers flights, rental cars, SIMs, and roughly what the trip cost.

Three things to book early

&Beyond Ngala — safari lodges book up six to nine months out, especially Nov – Apr.
Robben Island — ferry capacity is tight; weekday tours sell out a week in advance.
La Colombe — Cape Town's top tasting-menu restaurants release tables 2–3 months ahead.
