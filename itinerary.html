14 days on the ground · Nov 6 – Nov 20, 2017

The actual day-by-day.

A reference for pacing — not a script. Skip days, swap nights, do what you want.

Johannesburg, transit

Day 1Mon · Nov 6

Arrive Joburg · first dinner in Sandton

  • Land at O.R. Tambo (JNB) mid-morning after the long-haul flights. ~20-minute transfer to The Capital Empire in Sandton.
  • Settle, nap. Walk over to Nelson Mandela Square in the evening.
  • Dinner at Bukhara — the best curry in Sandton, on the square.

Greater Kruger · Safari

Day 2Tue · Nov 7

Joburg → Hoedspruit → Ngala

JNB → HDS on Airlink (~1 hr) → ~45-minute &Beyond transfer → check in at &Beyond Ngala Safari Lodge.
  • Lunch on arrival. Afternoon game drive at 3:30. Sundowners somewhere out in the reserve. Dinner back at the lodge.
Days 3–5Wed–Fri · Nov 8–10

Full safari days at Ngala

  • The lodge runs a daily rhythm: 5:30am wake-up, morning drive until ~10am, brunch, free time / nap / spa / pool, 3:30pm afternoon drive that continues after sunset with spotlights.
  • Open-air boma dinner in the courtyard or kraal — the staff usually sings.
  • Big Five sightings are routine over four days. Worth asking the head ranger about bush walks on at least one morning.
Day 6Sat · Nov 11

Ngala → Joburg · steakhouse night

Morning drive → brunch → transfer to Hoedspruit → HDS → JNB on Airlink (~1 hr) → The Capital Empire.
  • Dinner at The Butcher Shop & Grill on Nelson Mandela Square — South Africa is a beef country, and the dry-aged ribeye is the move.

Cape Town, part one

Day 7Sun · Nov 12

Joburg → Cape Town · Camps Bay base

JNB → CPT on British Airways (~2 hr) → ~30-minute transfer to Primi Seacastle in Camps Bay.
  • Walk the Camps Bay strip. Sunset on the beach with Table Mountain behind you.
  • Dinner uptown at Kloof Street House — outdoor deck under fairy lights.
Day 8Mon · Nov 13

Cape Point peninsula loop

  • 8am pickup for an 8-hour private guided day with Lynette at Exquisite Cape Tours — Chapman's Peak Drive, the Cape of Good Hope, Boulders penguins, Simon's Town lunch.
  • Back to Camps Bay for dinner.
Day 9Tue · Nov 14

Skeleton Gorge hike · Kirstenbosch

  • 7:20am pickup with Hike Table Mountain for the 5–6 hour up-Skeleton-Gorge / down-Nursery-Ravine loop.
  • Late lunch at the Kirstenbosch Tea Room.
  • Slow afternoon walking Kirstenbosch Botanical Garden — entry already included with the hike.
Day 10Wed · Nov 15

Stellenbosch winelands day

10-hour day trip with Wine Flies — four or five wineries through Stellenbosch and Franschhoek with cheese pairings, a long lunch, local guides. Door-to-door from the hotel.
  • Easy dinner back in Camps Bay (or a deck cocktail at The Bungalow in Clifton) before the early start tomorrow.

Garden Route

Day 11Thu · Nov 16

Cape Town → L'Agulhas → Knysna

Cape Town → L'Agulhas (~3 hr) → Mossel Bay → Knysna (~4 hr more) → Augusta Bay of Knysna. Pick up the rental in central Cape Town.
  • Lunch at Sea Shack Struisbaai — fish and chips at picnic tables on the dunes after a quick stop at the southernmost tip of Africa.
  • Knysna dinner at JJ's Restaurant on the Knysna Quays.
Day 12Fri · Nov 17

Knysna Heads · Plett wildlife · check in to Plett

  • Morning: Knysna Heads, Margaret's Viewpoint, brunch at East Head Café.
  • Midday drive east to The Crags. Afternoon at Monkeyland, Birds of Eden, and Jukani Wildlife Sanctuary — all three on one ticket loop.
  • Check in at Plett Beachfront Accommodation. Dinner at The Lookout Deck.
Day 13Sat · Nov 18

Robberg hike · drive back to Cape Town

  • Breakfast and pickup lunch from Clare's Cakes & Coffee.
  • 9:30am at Robberg Nature Reserve — the full 5-hour clifftop loop with seals and dolphins.
Plett → Cape Town (~6 hr) → check in at Fairways On The Bay back in Camps Bay.

Cape Town, part two

Day 14Sun · Nov 19

Drop the car · Bo-Kaap · Robben Island

  • 8:30am drop the rental in the CBD.
  • Walk Bo-Kaap's painted streets, slow it down with a guided neighborhood tour.
  • Lunch at the V&A Food Market.
  • 1pm Robben Island ferry from the V&A — 3.5-hour round-trip including the prison tour.
  • Dinner at The Codfather in Camps Bay.
Day 15Mon · Nov 20

Sundowner lunch · fly home

  • Slow morning at the hotel.
  • Noon: lunch at The Bungalow in Clifton — deck table, cocktails, the Atlantic.
  • Late evening: transfer to Cape Town International (CPT) for the long flight home.

If you only have 10 days

Skip Joburg as a stop (use it as a same-day transit only), and cut one Cape Town night. Keep the four safari nights and the Garden Route loop — both are the reason to come.

If you have 18+ days

Add the Whale Coast at Hermanus (Jun–Nov), a night at Tsitsikamma in the indigenous forest, or extend the safari with a second lodge in a different ecosystem (Phinda for sand-forest, the Cape's Karoo for arid rhino tracking).
