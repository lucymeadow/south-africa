Stellenbosch & the Winelands

A long day trip from Cape Town · four or five wineries, one long lunch.
Eikestad

Day trip from Cape Town Don't drive yourself ~10-hour day Summer is busy

Stellenbosch is South Africa's second-oldest European settlement — Cape Dutch gables under massive oaks, a university town's energy, art galleries, and the highest density of working wineries in the country. With Franschhoek and Paarl, it forms the three-cornered Cape Winelands region, all within an hour of Cape Town.

The simplest move from a Cape Town base is a guided day tour: a small van picks you up at the hotel around 8am, you taste at four or five estates with cheese pairings and a long lunch in the middle, and you're back in the city by 6pm. No one has to drive.

What we did

  • Do

    Wine Flies Wine Tours

    Ten-hour small-group day tour from Cape Town through four or five working wineries in Stellenbosch and Franschhoek — cheese, charcuterie, a sit-down lunch, local guides who know the winemakers. Door-to-door from the hotel. Choose between their fixed itineraries or customise.

If you'd rather drive yourself

  • See

    Stellenbosch (town)

    Park in the historic centre and walk Dorp Street under the oaks — Cape Dutch buildings, the Toy and Miniature Museum, a serious gallery scene. The old Volkskombuis and modern Rust en Vrede are dinner picks if you stay overnight.

  • See

    Stellenbosch · Franschhoek · Paarl

    The historic trio. Stellenbosch is the biggest with the deepest wine history; Franschhoek is the French-Huguenot one with the prettiest setting and the heaviest fine-dining scene (Le Quartier Français, La Colombe Franschhoek); Paarl is the quieter wine town with the Berg river running through it.

Wineries worth specifically asking your guide about

Delaire Graff — clifftop views, modern art, a serious lunch restaurant.
Tokara — sleek architecture and a long-running Cab Sauvignon.
Waterford Estate — the wine-and-chocolate pairing is overdone everywhere now; this is where it actually works.
Babylonstoren — the most beautiful single property in the country. Garden visit, then lunch.
Boschendal — old Cape Dutch homestead, casual deli-and-picnic-on-the-lawn option.
