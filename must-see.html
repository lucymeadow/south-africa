Must See

The shortlist
  • Kruger

    &Beyond Ngala Safari

    Four nights in the 14,700-hectare &Beyond Ngala Private Game Reserve, which shares an unfenced border with Kruger National Park. Two game drives a day with a ranger-and-tracker team, off-road traversing on the private concession, open-air boma dinners. The trip basically pays for itself in animal sightings. (See the safari page for what a day looks like.)

  • Cape Town

    Table Mountain

    The flat-topped icon of the city. You can ride the cableway up in clear weather, or hike one of the routes from Kirstenbosch — Hike Table Mountain runs guided trips up Skeleton Gorge that know the weather windows. Plan for a clear morning before the famous "tablecloth" of cloud rolls in.

  • Cape Town

    Cape of Good Hope

    The southwestern tip of Africa — wild fynbos cliffs, baboons in the road, the original Cape Point lighthouse. Best done as a full-day loop down the peninsula: Chapman's Peak Drive, Cape Point itself, the Boulders penguins, lunch in Simon's Town. Hire a local driver for the day.

  • Cape Town

    Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden

    Internationally rated among the world's great botanical gardens, on the eastern slopes of Table Mountain. The Boomslang treetop canopy walk, the indigenous proteas, the tea room for lunch. In summer (Nov–Mar) there are open-air Sunday sunset concerts. Pair with a hike up Skeleton Gorge, which starts here.

  • Cape Town

    Robben Island

    The prison where Nelson Mandela spent 18 of his 27 years. Half-day tour by ferry from the V&A Waterfront, with a guided walk through Section B and Mandela's cell. The guides are former political prisoners — that's the part no other tour can replicate. Book in advance: ferries fill a week or more out.

  • Cape Town

    Bo-Kaap

    The historic Cape Malay neighborhood on the slopes of Signal Hill — rows of cobblestone streets lined with vivid pink, blue, green, and yellow houses. Walk it on a Sunday morning; the Bo-Kaap Museum tells the story behind the color.

  • Stellenbosch

    Winelands Day Tour

    A small-group day tour through four or five working wineries in Stellenbosch and Franschhoek — cheese pairings, a long lunch, local guides. Booking with Wine Flies or similar means nobody has to drive. The wine is genuinely world-class and the prices are a fraction of California.

  • Garden Route

    Knysna Heads

    Two sandstone cliffs framing the narrow mouth of Knysna Lagoon — one of the most dramatic estuaries on the coast. Park at the East Head Café for the postcard view and a plate of local oysters.

  • Garden Route

    Robberg Nature Reserve

    Five-hour clifftop loop on a wild rocky peninsula outside Plettenberg Bay — dolphins below, fur seal colony at the point, fynbos all around. The signature Garden Route hike. Pack water, lunch, and good shoes.
